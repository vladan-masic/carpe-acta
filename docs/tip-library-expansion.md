# Tip library expansion: CA-041–CA-160

Added 120 newly authored bilingual entries on 2026-09-28, ten per requested
group, for 165 tips in total. These batches were a roadmap in the original chat;
they are new editorial content, not quotations or imports of finished chat tips.

The existing 45 entries retain their IDs, wording, effort, tags, and order.
New tips are appended in editorial-number order. CA numbers are an editorial
cross-reference; descriptive app IDs remain the stable keys for completion history.
The existing `studying` and `coding` IDs are reused with broader translated labels;
ten new category IDs cover the remaining groups. No completion or selection logic
changes. The six-item preview remains unchanged; the date-to-quest mapping changes
as expected when catalog length changes.

Every new entry has English and Serbian Latin title, advice, action, and explanation,
plus lowercase language-independent tags. Effort estimates the immediate action,
usually preparation or one small piece of work, not an entire project or routine.
Similar principles may appear in different contexts, but actions are tailored to the
specific obstacle rather than copies of the same generic start-small instruction.

## Editorial approach and references

Explanations describe plausible practical benefits, not guaranteed outcomes or
claims that each individual tip has been scientifically tested. Fear and low-energy
content focuses on everyday task management rather than diagnosis or treatment.
Physical activity content is gentle, adaptable, and does not prescribe intensity.
Explanations remain stored without adding an explanation UI.

References consulted for general principles (not sources of all 120 entries):

- [NHS: Tackling your to-do list](https://www.nhs.uk/every-mind-matters/mental-wellbeing-tips/self-help-cbt-techniques/tackling-your-to-do-list/): small commitments, practical reminders, choosing a workable time, and modest rewards.
- [CDC: Getting started with physical activity](https://www.cdc.gov/healthy-weight-growth/physical-activity/getting-started.html): begin gradually and choose suitable, enjoyable activities.
- [The Learning Scientists: retrieval practice](https://www.learningscientists.org/retrieval-practice) and [spacing and retrieval discussion](https://www.learningscientists.org/blog/2017/7/20-1): retrieving and revisiting learning, with attention to context and limits.
