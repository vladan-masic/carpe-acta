  they are not minimum durations for the habit itself.

  acknowledgement and practicing the sequence. Basis for A and D. We do not repeat
  the page's strong claims about speed of habit formation or brain changes.

Write original actions, examples and explanations in both languages. Do not copy
or closely translate book passages, branded worksheets, diagrams, covers or logos.

not jurisdiction-specific clearance or a conclusion about trademark rights.

### A — Acknowledge a Small Finish / Primeti mali uspeh

Proposed ID: `acknowledge-a-small-finish`; category: `habits`; effort: 1 minute.
Source: Tiny Habits, [celebration](https://tinyhabits.com/rewire/).

| Field | English | Serbian Latin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Text | Give a small action a moment of recognition. | Odvoji trenutak da primetiš malu završenu radnju. |
| Action | Put one loose note where it belongs. Immediately acknowledge the finish with a quiet gesture or a few encouraging words that feel natural to you. | Stavi jednu belešku na njeno mesto. Odmah primeti da je radnja završena, uz nenametljiv gest ili nekoliko ohrabrujućih reči koje ti prijaju. |
| Why | A brief acknowledgement can make a small finish feel worth noticing. | Kratko priznanje sebi može pomoći da primetiš vrednost malog završenog koraka. |

Distinct from selecting a later reward or recording a completion. No forced
enthusiasm, purchases, or claim that one repetition establishes a habit.

### B — Practice One Quality / Vežbaj jednu osobinu

Proposed ID: `practice-one-quality`; category: `habits`; effort: 2 minutes.
Source: Atomic Habits, [identity-based habits](https://jamesclear.com/identity-based-habits).

| Field | English | Serbian Latin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Text | Make a quality you value visible in one action. | Pokaži osobinu koju ceniš kroz jednu radnju. |
| Action | Choose a quality you want to practice today, such as care or curiosity. Spend one minute acting on it: check one detail in your work or write one question about it. Name the quality that action expressed. | Izaberi osobinu koju danas želiš da vežbaš, poput pažljivosti ili radoznalosti. Posveti joj jedan minut: proveri jedan detalj u svom radu ili napiši jedno pitanje o njemu. Imenuj osobinu koju ta radnja pokazuje. |
| Why | A concrete example connects an intention about who you want to be with something you can do now. | Konkretan primer povezuje nameru o tome kakva osoba želiš da budeš sa nečim što možeš da uradiš sada. |

Distinct from listing old achievements. This is practice, not a verdict about
character; a missed action does not invalidate the quality.

### C — Add Enjoyment to the Task / Dodaj nešto prijatno zadatku

Proposed ID: `add-enjoyment-to-the-task`; category: `low-motivation`; effort: 2 minutes.
Source: Atomic Habits, [temptation bundling](https://jamesclear.com/temptation-bundling).

| Field | English | Serbian Latin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Text | Try making routine work more pleasant while you do it. | Probaj da rutinski posao učiniš prijatnijim dok ga radiš. |
| Action | Play a familiar instrumental track you enjoy and spend one minute putting loose stationery away. Notice whether the music makes this simple task easier to approach; switch it off if it distracts you. | Pusti poznatu instrumentalnu numeru koju voliš i jedan minut vraćaj razbacani pribor za pisanje na mesto. Primeti da li ti muzika olakšava da pristupiš ovom jednostavnom zadatku; isključi je ako ti odvlači pažnju. |
| Why | Pairing a routine task with something enjoyable can make the experience more inviting. | Povezivanje rutinskog zadatka sa nečim prijatnim može učiniti rad privlačnijim. |

Distinct from a reward after finishing. This is one bounded pairing experiment,
not a restriction on access to rest, food, or other needs. Credit Clear's explanation,
not sole invention of the concept.

## Proposed implementation boundary after review

Use a separate typed source registry and a mapping keyed by existing `TipId`,
rather than adding author names to category IDs or duplicating the tip catalog.
A reference should contain source ID, exact technique/article URL, relationship
(`inspired-by` or `related-reading`), and bilingual explanation. Allow multiple
references. Store draft provenance only in this document until content is approved.

A source filter should affect Browse tips only, not random or daily pools.
Render source information consistently wherever full tip details appear. Use
native disclosure controls, descriptive links, visible keyboard focus, and
readable light/dark styling. Future review screenshots should use light mode.

Adding A–C would make the catalog 180 entries; adding D too would make it 181.
The current daily function indexes by day seed modulo catalog length, so even
append-only additions change date-to-tip mapping. Do not append them as part of
source-label work. Choose separately between:

1. Source metadata first; keep all 177 tips and the existing daily function.
2. Approved new tips, with explicit acceptance of the changed daily mapping.
3. A separately designed, tested daily-pool policy before introducing new tips.

Recommendation: review A–C, then implement attribution independently. Do not
silently rewrite older tips, reserve live IDs, change random eligibility, or
introduce a new daily-selection policy during that implementation.
