# Botanical details

Asset: `src/assets/illustrations/botanical-details.webp` (600 × 200, transparent WebP, 32 KB). Generated using the built-in imagegen tool; three separate cells displayed through CSS background positioning. Decorative only. Original retained in the image-generation output folder.

Prompt:

Create one transparent botanical sprite sheet, landscape 3:1, divided invisibly into THREE equal square cells in a single horizontal row. Each cell contains one distinct tiny delicate hand-painted watercolor botanical detail, centered with ample transparent margin and entirely within its cell. Left cell: a slender orange wildflower with two muted olive leaves. Middle cell: a small curved sage-green leafy sprig. Right cell: a loose upward-growing cluster of meadow grasses with a few tiny cream and ochre flowers. Warm countryside storybook aesthetic, fine imperfect brown ink lines, translucent watercolor washes, muted greens and terracotta. Airy sparse composition readable at 60px tall. Actual transparent background, no ground, no paper rectangle, no lettering, no frames, no shadows. These are small editorial margin decorations for an illustrated paper website, not bold icons.

Annotations use a self-hosted Caveat 500 subset with ASCII letters, period, space, and č ć š ž đ Č Ć Š Ž Đ. Source: Google Fonts Caveat v23; license in `src/assets/fonts/Caveat-OFL.txt`. No runtime font-service request.
